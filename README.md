🍿 CINEVIE+ Online Ticketing – Business Analyst & Project Manager Report
This repository contains the requirement analysis and project documentation for a team project focused on designing an online movie ticket booking platform.
I worked as a Project Manager & Business Analyst, applying software project management techniques to produce a structured, comprehensive project plan and deliverables.

🔍 **Project Overview**

Duration: May 2025 – July 2025

Role: Project Manager & Business Analyst (Student Project)

Objective: Analyze business requirements, plan project activities, manage risks, and document a complete solution for CineVie+ Online Ticketing.

📋 **Key Contributions**

Requirement Gathering & Analysis:

Identified current business problems, customer needs, and project objectives.

Proposed & compared three solution approaches (SaaS, custom development, Odoo Community) based on cost, risks, and benefits.

Selected the optimal solution: custom web development for flexibility and scalability.

Project Planning:

Built a Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) and network diagram.

Designed a Gantt chart for all project phases: Initiating – Planning – Executing – Monitoring & Controlling – Closing.

Estimated budget for HR, infrastructure, training, and maintenance.

Prepared RACI chart & Responsibility Assignment Matrix (RAM) for accountability.

Risk Management:

Identified & prioritized risks, assessed impacts, and proposed mitigation strategies.

Communication & Procurement Planning:

Created a stakeholder communication matrix, meeting guidelines, and communication standards.

Defined procurement list, contracts, acceptance criteria, and deployment schedule.

Deliverables:

BRD & SRS describing business and software requirements.

Project plan, Gantt chart, budget breakdown, risk report.

🛠 **Tools & Technologies**

Documentation & Planning: Microsoft Word, Excel

Scheduling: Gantt chart tools

Collaboration: Team-based planning & review meetings
